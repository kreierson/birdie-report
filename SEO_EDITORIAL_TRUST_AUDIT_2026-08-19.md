## Publication rule going forward

Every new product review, comparison, or buyer's guide must declare one `review_basis` value. A hands-on or hybrid label should be used only when the named author can substantiate the direct-use details stated in the article. Research-based coverage should cite current primary sources where available and should not invent measurements, ownership, fitting sessions, rounds played, or first-person product experience.
