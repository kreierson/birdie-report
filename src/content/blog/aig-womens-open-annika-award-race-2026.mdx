The **2026 AIG Women's Open** is not just deciding the final women's major of the year.

It is also carrying one of the cleanest season-long side fights in golf.

According to the **LPGA's official July 29, 2026 update**, checked again on **Thursday, July 30**, only **Nelly Korda** and **Haeran Ryu** remain in contention for the **Rolex ANNIKA Major Award**. The same LPGA breakdown says **Korda leads Ryu by six points** entering **Royal Lytham & St Annes**.

That is a lot better than rolling into the week with the award basically wrapped in plastic.

This piece is based on the LPGA's official **July 29, 2026** ANNIKA award update, checked on **July 30**. No pretending I built the points table myself in a media center while somebody handed me a coffee and a credential lanyard.

If you want the major-week setup first, read [our current AIG Women's Open field breakdown](/blog/aig-womens-open-field-50th-edition-2026/), [the how-to-watch guide](/blog/aig-womens-open-2026-how-to-watch-royal-lytham/), and [our opinion on why this championship does not need one designated main character](/blog/aig-womens-open-no-single-main-character-opinion-2026/).

## The Whole Race Comes Down to Two Players

The LPGA says the eligibility rule is what trimmed this thing down.

To win the **Rolex ANNIKA Major Award**, a player has to win at least one major in the season. That leaves only the two women who split the first four majors:

- **Korda** won the **Chevron Championship**
- **Korda** won the **U.S. Women's Open**
- **Korda** also logged a **tie for eighth** at the **KPMG Women's PGA Championship**
- **Ryu** won the **KPMG Women's PGA Championship**
- **Ryu** won **The Amundi Evian Championship**

That is a proper heavyweight setup.

We already covered [Korda's Hall of Fame chase](/blog/nelly-korda-hall-of-fame-chase-opinion-2026/) and [Ryu's second straight major win at Evian](/blog/haeran-ryu-wins-evian-second-straight-major-2026/). The useful update now is that those strands are not just sitting beside each other anymore. They are directly colliding this week.

## Royal Lytham Can Decide More Than One Trophy

The LPGA's official note makes the stakes even cleaner.

If either **Korda** or **Ryu** wins the **AIG Women's Open**, she would become the **first player ever to claim the Rolex ANNIKA Major Award on the back of three major wins in one season**.

That is not a side stat. That is serious season-shaping history.

The LPGA also notes:

- **Korda** would become only the **second two-time winner** of the award, joining **Minjee Lee**
- **Ryu** would become the **fourth Korean winner**
- **Ryu** would also become the **fifth player** to win both the **Rolex ANNIKA Major Award** and the **Louise Suggs Rolex Rookie of the Year** during her career

Again: not background noise.

## Korda Has the Lead, but This Is Not a Lock

The most important number in the LPGA piece is still the simplest one:

**six points**.

That is Korda's cushion.

It means she has control. It does not mean she gets to coast.

The LPGA published full clinching scenarios, and the broad point is obvious even before you get lost in the spreadsheet weeds: **Ryu still has a real path**. The official scenario rundown stretches all the way down to permutations where **Ryu can still steal the award with an eighth-place finish if Korda slides far enough down the board**.

That matters because it keeps the week from turning into a polite coronation.

Golf is better when a major also drags a real season race into the same arena. Women's golf has actually been pretty good at that lately. We saw it in [Jenny Shin's Scottish Open win heading into Royal Lytham](/blog/jenny-shin-wins-womens-scottish-open-2026/), in [Lottie Woad refusing to stay boxed inside "future star" language](/blog/lottie-woad-second-lpga-win-opinion-2026/), and in the bigger point we made when [the AIG finally started acting like a proper major](/blog/aig-womens-open-is-acting-like-a-major-2026/).

This is another version of the same thing:

the tour has a major that actually feels connected to the season around it.

## Bottom Line

The **LPGA's July 29, 2026** update says only **Nelly Korda** and **Haeran Ryu** can still win the **Rolex ANNIKA Major Award**, with **Korda taking a six-point lead** into the **AIG Women's Open** at **Royal Lytham & St Annes**.

That gives this major one more thing it absolutely did not need, but happily gets anyway:

another serious reason to matter.
