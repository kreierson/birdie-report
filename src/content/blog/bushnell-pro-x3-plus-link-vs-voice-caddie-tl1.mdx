- **wind in the display**

- **home-elevation adjustment**

## This Is Really Premium Feel Versus Premium Feature Ceiling

That is the cleanest way to think about the comparison.

The **TL1** is trying to give you a nicer premium-feeling rangefinder experience for meaningfully less money.

The **Pro X3+LINK** is trying to give you the most feature-loaded laser Bushnell can build.

Those are different jobs.

If what you want is a premium-feeling laser that looks good, reads quickly, and does not ask you to defend a five-hundred-dollar receipt to yourself, the **TL1** is the stronger answer.

If what you want is Bushnell's fullest compensated-distance tool and you already know the extra features are not wasted on you, the **Pro X3+LINK** is still the stronger flagship.
