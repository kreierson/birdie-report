## Gifts by golfer type

Same picks as above. No extra products.

### Beginner

A dozen they can lose, a putter that forgives a miss, and yardages that do not need a manual. The Soft Feel and the Kirkland cover the ball. The Buckingham and the DFX Seven cover the putter. The GS24 is the cheap laser. The S12 is the button watch. The Smart Ball and the alignment sticks are the practice gifts that do not pretend to be a lesson.

### High handicapper

Stay with the mallet putters. The high-handicap putter guide is explicit that a mallet resists a miss the way a blade does not, which is why the Newport 2 is a splurge for a different golfer. Pair a Buckingham or a DFX with the Kirkland if they lose balls, and throw in the groove brush. The SM10 is the wrong "nice wedge" unless they already play a players' wedge.

### Gadget lover

The PRO L2 is the sensible laser. The Blue Tees Series 3 Max is the under-$200 step up, with the slower lock the rangefinder guide already flags. The S12 is the simple watch, not the map watch. The Tour Hybrid is the one-device splurge when they are tired of carrying two. The Mevo+ is only a gift if the room in that launch-monitor guide actually exists.

### Walker

The towel and the umbrella are the small walking gifts. The Coronado v3 is the budget shoe, with the wet-traction limit from the walking-shoe guide. The Founders Club is the $90 "try walking" cart. The CaddyLite is the $160 cart if they already know they will walk. A cart bag is a rider gift. Use the Cart 14 or the Cart Lite only if the bag is going on a cart.
