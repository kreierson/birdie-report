## FutureFit33 Is More Interesting Than Most Max-Head Tuning

One thing Cobra does better than a lot of max-head competitors is admit that golfers still need fit help even when the head is designed to be broadly friendly.

The **FutureFit33** sleeve gives you loft and lie tuning independently across a much wider set of combinations than the usual "turn it up or down and hope" hosel story.

That does not mean every golfer should spend three weeks pretending to be their own fitter.

It means the head has more room to be dialed in if:

- launch is a little too flat
- the face needs a touch more upright help
- you want to calm a miss without bailing on the whole club

That is a real selling point, especially at this price.
