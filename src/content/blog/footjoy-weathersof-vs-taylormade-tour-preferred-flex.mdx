Buy the **FootJoy WeatherSof** if:

Buy the **TaylorMade Tour Preferred Flex** if:

For most golfers whose first filter is price, the **WeatherSof** is still the smarter answer.

For golfers who already know they want a nicer glove but do not want to pay top-shelf premium prices, the **Tour Preferred Flex** is the smarter answer.
