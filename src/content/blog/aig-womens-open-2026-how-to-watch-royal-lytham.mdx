If you are going to block out time for the **2026 AIG Women's Open**, at least do it with the right windows.

According to the **LPGA's official how-to-watch page published Monday, July 27, 2026**, checked on **Tuesday, July 28**, the championship at **Royal Lytham & St Annes** will air in the United States across **Golf Channel**, **USA Network**, and **NBC** from **Thursday, July 30, through Sunday, August 2**.

The **R&A's current championship page**, also checked on **July 28**, says this is the **50th staging** of the event, with **144 players** competing for a **record $10 million purse**. It also lists **Miyu Yamashita** as the defending champion after her win at **Royal Porthcawl** in **2025**.

This piece is based on the official **LPGA watch guide published July 27, 2026**, plus the **R&A's live AIG Women's Open page**, both checked on **Tuesday, July 28, 2026**. No pretending I got handed a production sheet in some TV compound behind the range.

## The Practical TV Schedule

Here is the part most people actually need:

- **Thursday, July 30:** **4-8 a.m. ET** on **Golf Channel**, then **8 a.m.-2 p.m. ET** on **USA Network**
- **Friday, July 31:** **4-8 a.m. ET** on **Golf Channel**, then **8 a.m.-2 p.m. ET** on **USA Network**
- **Saturday, August 1:** **7 a.m.-12 p.m. ET** on **USA Network**, then **12-2 p.m. ET** on **NBC**
- **Sunday, August 2:** **7 a.m.-12 p.m. ET** on **USA Network**, then **12-2 p.m. ET** on **NBC**

That is a much cleaner setup than the usual "check in late and hope the leaders have not already done the interesting part" routine.
