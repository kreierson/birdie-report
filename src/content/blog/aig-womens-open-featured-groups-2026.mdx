The **LPGA** did not exactly tiptoe into the **2026 AIG Women's Open** with the featured groups.

It basically went straight to the good stuff.

According to the **LPGA's official featured-groups release published Wednesday, July 29, 2026**, checked on **Thursday, July 30**, the championship at **Royal Lytham & St Annes** opens with a top-line trio of **Lydia Ko**, **Nelly Korda**, and **Haeran Ryu**, then keeps stacking serious names behind them.

This piece is based on the LPGA's official **July 29, 2026 featured-groups story**, checked on **July 30**. No pretending I was in the room when somebody started arranging names on a whiteboard and decided to be rude to the rest of the field.

If you want the broader setup first, read [our full AIG Women's Open field breakdown](/blog/aig-womens-open-field-50th-edition-2026/), [the ANNIKA award-race update](/blog/aig-womens-open-annika-award-race-2026/), and [our watch guide for the week](/blog/aig-womens-open-2026-how-to-watch-royal-lytham/).

## The Headline Group Is the One It Should Be

The first marquee tee time the LPGA highlighted is:

- **Thursday, July 30, 8:42 a.m., 1st tee**
- **Friday, July 31, 1:26 p.m., 1st tee**
- **Lydia Ko, Nelly Korda, Haeran Ryu**

That group works because every player brings a different kind of weight.

The LPGA notes that:

- **Ko** won this championship in **2024** at **St Andrews**
- **Korda** is still carrying the **Hall of Fame** pressure line we already covered [here](/blog/nelly-korda-hall-of-fame-chase-opinion-2026/)
- **Ryu** arrives chasing a **third straight major win** after the run we covered in [our Evian story](/blog/haeran-ryu-wins-evian-second-straight-major-2026/)

That is not a gimmick pairing. That is a weekly-news cycle by itself.

## The Defending-Champ Group Is No Joke Either

The next big one the LPGA flagged:

- **Thursday, July 30, 1:15 p.m., 1st tee**
- **Friday, July 31, 8:31 a.m., 1st tee**
- **Miyu Yamashita, Jeeno Thitikul, Lauren Coughlin**

That is a sneaky nasty group.

The official LPGA release says **Yamashita** returns as defending champion after her **2025 win at Royal Porthcawl**. It also points out **Thitikul** was the low amateur at this championship in both **2018** and **2019**, which is exactly the kind of stat that reminds you she has been orbiting this event for a while. **Coughlin**, meanwhile, brings the links credentials of a **2024 Women's Scottish Open win** and a **tie for 13th** at last year's AIG.

That trio has a very strong "everybody here knows how to survive a proper week" energy.

## The Hull Group Feels Like Prime British-Open-Theater Material

The LPGA's third highlighted group:

- **Thursday, July 30, 1:26 p.m., 1st tee**
- **Friday, July 31, 8:42 a.m., 1st tee**
- **Charley Hull, Hannah Green, Brooke Henderson**

This one has a little more chaos to it in the best way.

The LPGA says **Hull** has twice finished runner-up at the **AIG Women's Open**, including last year. **Green** has not had much joy in this major historically, which is exactly why she becomes interesting if she gets going early. **Henderson** still has a live **career-grand-slam** angle hanging around, and the LPGA notes she was **tied for 11th** the last time the event came to **Royal Lytham** in **2018**.

So yes, this group feels like broadcast producers trying to guarantee themselves something useful by lunchtime.

## The Tee Sheet Matches the Week

This is really the broader point.

The LPGA did not need to manufacture intrigue here. The week already had it.

We already argued in [our no-single-main-character opinion piece](/blog/aig-womens-open-no-single-main-character-opinion-2026/) that this major is better because it is not built around one lone superstar and a pile of supporting furniture. The featured groups back that up.

You have:

- **Ko, Korda, Ryu** for raw résumé weight
- **Yamashita, Thitikul, Coughlin** for form and recent links credibility
- **Hull, Green, Henderson** for major scars, home pressure, and legacy angles

That is a strong board before anybody even holes a putt.

## Bottom Line

The LPGA's official **July 29, 2026 featured-groups release**, checked on **July 30**, opens the **AIG Women's Open** with **Lydia Ko, Nelly Korda, and Haeran Ryu**, then follows with **Miyu Yamashita, Jeeno Thitikul, Lauren Coughlin** and **Charley Hull, Hannah Green, Brooke Henderson**.

That is exactly how a major should look:

stacked early, stacked late, and not especially interested in easing into the week.
