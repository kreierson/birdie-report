# Titleist GT2 vs Ping G440 Max Driver: Better Speed or Better Forgiveness?

If you are choosing between the **Titleist GT2** and **PING G440 Max**, the real decision is no longer “which brand makes the hotter driver?” It is this:

- **GT2:** the prior-generation value play with Titleist's excellent hosel fitting system, a standard 45.5-inch build, and a clean shape.
- **G440 Max:** the current forgiveness-first option with a longer stock build and movable back weighting.

For most golfers whose contact wanders around the face, I would start the fitting with the **G440 Max**. If you find a properly fit GT2 at a meaningful discount and prefer its shape, sound, or standard length, it can be the smarter buy.

This is a research-based comparison, not a fake “I hit 400 balls with both” story. The specifications below were rechecked on August 19, 2026 against the official [Titleist GT2 page](https://www.titleist.com/golf-clubs/drivers/gt2) and [PING G440 Max page](https://ping.com/en-us/golf-clubs/drivers/g440-max-driver).

## First: GT2 and GTS2 Are Not the Same Driver

Titleist has since released the **GTS2**, but the **GT2** remains a distinct previous-generation model. That matters because search listings and used-club pages make the names ridiculously easy to confuse.

If you want the current-generation comparison, read [Titleist GTS2 vs PING G440 Max](/blog/titleist-gts2-vs-ping-g440-max-driver/). Stay here if you are comparing a discounted or used **GT2** against a **G440 Max**.

|---|---|---|
| **Head size** | 460cc | 460cc |
| **Lofts** | 8°, 9°, 10°, 11° | 9°, 10.5°, 12° |
| **Standard length** | 45.5 inches | 46 inches with ALTA CB Blue 50; 45.5 with several other stock shafts |

| **Current price** | Prior-generation inventory varies | Current retail varies; verify before buying |

Do not make this decision from a stale list price. GT2 value depends entirely on the actual head, shaft, condition, and return policy in front of you.

## The Contact Test Matters More Than Handicap

The lazy version of this comparison says low handicappers should buy Titleist and high handicappers should buy PING. That is not useful.

Use your **strike pattern** instead:

- A tight pattern near the center gives you room to choose on launch, spin, shape, feel, and price.
- A wide heel-to-toe pattern makes the G440 Max's forgiveness-first design more valuable.
- A consistent heel or toe pattern means both heads need a fitting adjustment before you blame the model.

Spray the face, hit at least ten normal-speed balls with each build, and compare the **bad eight**, not the best two. Golf stores love selling the longest single drive. Scorecards care about the shot you hit after missing the center by half an inch.

## Launch, Spin, and Shaft Fit

GT2 offers 8° through 11° lofts and Titleist's SureFit system. That gives a fitter plenty of room to change loft and lie independently and pair the head with a broad shaft menu.

PING offers the G440 Max in 9°, 10.5°, and 12°, with ±1.5° loft adjustment. Its standard length changes with the shaft: the ALTA CB Blue 50 build is 46 inches, while several heavier stock options are 45.5.

That half inch is not trivia. A longer club can create speed, but it can also spread impact around the face. If the 46-inch PING looks great on the spec sheet and terrible on face spray, test it at 45.5 before abandoning the head.

## Adjustability: Two Different Jobs

Titleist's SureFit hosel is the stronger tool when loft and lie are the main fitting questions. The interchangeable rear weight helps a fitter dial head weight and swing weight, but it is not a draw/fade slider.

PING combines hosel adjustment with a movable rear weight. That gives the fitter another way to influence shot shape and stability. It can help a repeatable miss; it does not magically erase an open face or an out-to-in path.

If the miss is a slice, fix the delivery too. Start with our [slice diagnosis and drills](/blog/how-to-fix-a-slice-for-good/) before paying premium-driver money to hide a swing problem.

## Who Should Buy the Titleist GT2?

Choose the GT2 when:

- the fitted build produces equal or better dispersion;
- you prefer the 45.5-inch standard length and Titleist address shape;
- the SureFit loft/lie matrix solves your launch window cleanly;
- the actual price is meaningfully below a current flagship; and
- the seller gives you enough return protection to verify the fit.

Do not buy it merely because “Titleist is for better players.” Titleist itself positions GT2 as a forgiving, high-MOI design. The case is fit plus value, not ego.

## Who Should Buy the PING G440 Max?

Choose the G440 Max when:

- your contact moves across the face;
- your worst drives lose too much launch or curve offline;
- movable rear weighting improves a repeatable directional miss;
- the 12° option or lighter high-launch build fits your speed; or
- you want the simpler forgiveness-first starting point.

PING calls MAX the most forgiving G440 model. That does not guarantee it wins your fitting, but it makes it the logical first head for the golfer who wants fewer disastrous outcomes.

## A Five-Minute Fitting Scorecard

Ignore the longest ball and record these for each build:

| Check | What wins |
|---|---|
| **Strike pattern** | Tighter cluster on face spray |
| **Carry consistency** | Smallest gap between best and worst useful shots |
| **Left/right dispersion** | Narrowest playable window |
| **Launch and spin** | Stable window for your speed, not one perfect number |
| **Confidence** | Head and sound you can commit to under pressure |
| **Real cost** | Head, correct shaft, fitting, and return policy combined |

## Verdict

**Best default recommendation: PING G440 Max.** Its entire design is aimed at speed plus forgiveness across a broader strike pattern, and its movable rear weight gives a fitter another useful lever.

**Best value recommendation: Titleist GT2, but only at the right price and fit.** It remains a legitimate 460cc speed-and-forgiveness head with excellent SureFit adjustability. The arrival of GTS2 did not make GT2 bad; it made overpaying for GT2 harder to justify.
