## Why Kirkland Wins the Value Argument So Hard

Costco's current listing is still absurd in the funniest possible way.

For about **$34.04**, you get:

- a **3-piece** golf ball
- a **urethane cover**
- a **24-count** pack
- a **338-dimple** aerodynamic pattern
- a product Costco still positions around **greenside control** and a **penetrating ball flight**

That is not bargain-bin two-piece range-ball nonsense.

That is a real consumer-performance golf ball sold with warehouse-store pricing logic instead of golf-industry pricing logic.

And that is why this comparison matters.

Once the per-ball math gets this lopsided, TaylorMade cannot win on price. It can only win on product preference.

If you are the kind of buyer who wants the simplest name-brand urethane answer and does not want to think about Costco memberships, restocks, or whether your ball purchase came from the same place as patio furniture and protein bars, **Tour Response** is very easy to understand.

This is where the whole comparison stops being polite.

TaylorMade starts the current **Tour Response** listing at **$42.99** for a dozen.

Costco is currently listing **Kirkland Signature Performance Plus** at about **$34.04 for 24 balls**.

So the real comparison is not:

- $34 versus $43

It is closer to:

- about **$17 per dozen equivalent** for Kirkland
- **$42.99 per dozen** for Tour Response

That is a huge gap.

So if your first filter is simple value, the article is over. **Kirkland wins.**

The only reason to keep reading is if you think the TaylorMade product story is better enough to justify the extra money.

## Feel and Product Personality: Tour Response Is Easier to Like

This is the category where **Tour Response** gets its best chance to fight back.

TaylorMade explicitly leans into:

- soft feel
- consistency
- approachable urethane performance

That is a stronger emotional product pitch than Costco's.

Kirkland's listing is much more basic. It tells you the ball is urethane, three-piece, and built for distance plus greenside control. Useful, yes. Romantic, not exactly.

If you shop golf balls by how they are supposed to **feel** and by how clearly the brand positions them inside a proper lineup, **Tour Response** has the better case.

If you shop golf balls by whether the receipt makes you laugh in a good way, **Kirkland** keeps the edge.

- **Long** driver distance

That is a very usable mainstream fit profile.

So if you want the more fully explained mainstream product fit, **Tour Response** wins that category.
