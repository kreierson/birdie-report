**Planning a SentryWorld golf trip? Start with the calendar.** The 2026 season has ended, the course will be closed for the **2027 golf season**, and the official rates page currently lists **2028 prices and booking dates as TBD**. Do not build a 2027 golf itinerary around a round here.

This is a **research-based planning review**, checked against official sources on October 7, 2026. It covers the golf course in Stevens Point, Wisconsin, not a firsthand round or a hotel stay. We have not played the renovated course or measured its conditions, pace, or service.

## Is SentryWorld worth considering?

The appeal is a public round built around convenience: golf, practice, a cart, and on-course refreshments packaged together. That can suit a group that wants fewer separate purchases during the day. Whether it is worth the eventual price depends on the published offer and what your group will actually use.

For now, keep it on a future shortlist rather than treating it as a bookable golf stop. A great-sounding package is no help if the course is closed.

## What the 2027 closure means for your trip

[SentryWorld's renovation announcement](https://www.sentryworld.com/newsroom/2027-golf-course-renovation) says the course will close for the entire 2027 golf season. Work includes irrigation, bunkers, selected greens and tees, and practice facilities, ahead of future USGA championships.

The announcement says **The Inn, PJ's restaurant, banquet facilities, and fieldhouse will remain open** during the course closure. A hotel reservation therefore does not establish that golf is available. If golf is the reason for your trip, verify the playing dates separately before committing to lodging or travel.

The [official trip-planning FAQ](https://www.sentryworld.com/golf/plan-your-golf-trip) identifies the end of the 2026 season. Older reviews describe the pre-renovation course; they cannot establish how the finished layout will play.

## Rates and booking: what is known now

The [official tee-time and rates page](https://www.sentryworld.com/golf/tee-times-and-rates) now lists both the fully inclusive day-guest rate and booking dates as **TBD for 2028**. The $375 figure previously discussed in this review was a 2026 rate, not a current offer or a confirmed future price.

That page still describes a 90-day day-guest booking window and preferred access through Stay and Play packages. Do not use that general rule to calculate a guaranteed reopening date or assume 2028 reservations are available. Wait for specific dates and terms.

Before paying, resolve three separate questions:

- **Can we play on our dates?** Get confirmed course availability, not just room availability.
- **What is the total price?** Check the published golf offer, lodging if needed, and any optional extras.
- **What happens if the group changes?** Read the applicable cancellation terms before collecting everyone's money.

## What the inclusive format offers

SentryWorld's [description of its inclusive golf experience](https://www.sentryworld.com/blog/fully-inclusive-golf-experience) explains the appeal: practice access, a GPS-equipped cart, on-course food and beverages, and 20-minute tee-time spacing. Those are the operator's described features, not our observation of actual pace or service.

The format may suit golfers who value an unhurried schedule and fewer on-course transactions. It offers less personal value if you would normally spend little on refreshments. Compare the full trip cost against what you would otherwise buy, rather than subtracting an invented food-and-drink allowance from the green fee. Recheck inclusions when the future offer is published.

## Policies to check before committing

The [published golf policies](https://www.sentryworld.com/policies) currently require at least 30 days' notice for cancellations or player-count reductions to receive a refund on paid rounds. They also distinguish rain from an actual course closure: rain alone may not stop play; weather closures result in rain checks for unfinished holes rather than refunds.

Treat these as the policies visible on our source-check date, and confirm the terms attached to your eventual booking. Do not assume a hotel cancellation policy also governs golf.

For questions about availability or the offer, use the [official contact page](https://www.sentryworld.com/contact-us). We have not contacted the resort or obtained a private reopening commitment.
