## Why the P790 Still Has a Very Real Case

The **P790** is not just prettier. TaylorMade's current official page gives it a stronger technical story than the usual "premium look, trust us" routine.

TaylorMade says the 2025 **P790** adds:

- an **ultra-high strength 4340M forged face**
- a face material **20 percent stronger** than the prior generation
- a sweet spot up to **24 percent larger**
- **SpeedFoam Air**
- **FLTD CG**
- a **thinner top line**
- an **increased sole radius**
- a **progressive leading edge**

That is a strong recipe if you want a players-distance iron that still gives you meaningful help.

The **P790** makes sense for golfers who want:

- a more compact premium look
- better turf-and-address appeal
- a cleaner set identity
- speed help without going full GI visually

That golfer exists.

They just should not automatically assume they are every golfer.

Mizuno is still leaning hard on the "world-renowned Mizuno feel" identity, just inside a Hot Metal platform built for distance and help instead of forged-purist romance.
