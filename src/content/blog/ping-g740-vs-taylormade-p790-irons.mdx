## Why the P790 Gets the Recommendation

The **P790** wins this page because it sits in the better middle ground for most golfers intentionally comparing these two.

TaylorMade's current official page still frames the club around:

- a larger sweet spot
- premium forged feel
- optimal launch with improved gapping
- a thinner topline
- increased sole radius
- progressive leading edge shaping
- **mid-high** forgiveness inside TaylorMade's own iron-family comparison

That matters because it means the **P790** is not a punishment iron.

It is the premium answer for golfers who still want help.

If your game is improving and you want a set that:

- still launches easily
- still gives you forgiveness
- looks meaningfully sharper than a full GI set
- feels like something you can keep for years

the **P790** makes more sense than the **G740** pretty quickly.

## Why the G740 Still Has a Very Real Job

The **G740** is not here to be aspirational.

It is here to make golf easier.

PING's current product page keeps pounding the same theme:

- more forgiveness
- more consistency
- higher launch
- wider sole
- longer blade length
- thicker topline

That is not subtle, and it should not be.

If you are still the golfer who:

- loses speed on misses too easily
- fights low launch
- needs more help in the long irons
- wants fewer rounds wrecked by random iron contact

the **G740** has the cleaner practical argument.

Sometimes the more useful iron is also the less glamorous iron. Golf is rude that way.

## Forgiveness: Ping Still Wins the Ugly-Shot Category

This is the part where the **G740** punches back hardest.

The **P790** is forgiving for a players-distance iron.

The **G740** is forgiving in a way that is designed to make worse swings less expensive.

Those are different standards.

The Ping's shape and category intent make it the better answer if you still need the clubhead to do a lot of rescue work on:

- toe strikes
- heel strikes
- low-face misses
- launch-starved long irons

If that is your golf, stop trying to buy the prettier answer just because it photographs better.

## Shape, Feel, and Long-Term Fit: TaylorMade Wins the Ceiling

The **P790** does not need to beat Ping at rescue. It needs to beat Ping at long-term usefulness for the improving golfer.

That is exactly what it does.

The TaylorMade case is simple:

- cleaner shape
- stronger premium feel story
- easier bag appeal
- more natural path for golfers trending lower

The **P790** is also easier to defend as a "buy once and keep them for a while" set if you are already progressing beyond obvious game-improvement visuals.

That does not mean everyone should buy it.

It means it wins when the golfer is ready.
