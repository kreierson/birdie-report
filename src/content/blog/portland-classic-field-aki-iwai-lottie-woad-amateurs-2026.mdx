The **Standard Portland Classic** is not sneaking onto the schedule this week with some sleepy little afterthought field.

It is showing up with a defending champion people should care about, a bunch of top-end LPGA names, and a pretty nasty amateur sub-plot too.

Official **LPGA** field and featured-groups coverage checked on **Thursday, August 13, 2026** says the **54th playing** of the **Portland Classic** gets underway at **Columbia Edgewater Country Club** with a **$2 million purse** and a **144-player field**. The same official coverage says defending champion **Aki Iwai** returns at **No. 19 in the Rolex Rankings**, with **nine players from the top 25**, **seven 2026 LPGA winners**, **21 rookies**, and three high-end amateur exemptions led by **Maria Jose Marin**, **Farah O'Keefe**, and **Valentine Delon**.

That is a lot more substance than "nice bridge week after the AIG."

This piece is based on the **LPGA's August 11 field breakdown**, **August 12 featured-groups post**, and the current **how-to-watch page**, all checked on **Thursday, August 13, 2026**. No pretending I was wandering Columbia Edgewater at sunrise with a coffee and a credential.

## Aki Iwai Is the Cleanest Starting Point

The official LPGA watch page puts **Aki Iwai** right at the center of the week, which makes sense.

She is the defending champion.
She is inside the **Rolex top 20**.
And according to the LPGA's current field story, she already has **seven top-10 finishes** this season even though she is still chasing her first win of **2026**.

That makes Portland a pretty good checkpoint week for her.

The LPGA's recap of last year's result says she ran away with the title using weekend rounds of **64** and **66** to win by **four**. This time she comes back with more status, more expectations, and more people in the field who can actually punch back.

## This Field Is Better Than the Lazy Framing Around It

The most useful number in the LPGA field breakdown is not just the purse or the player count.

It is the quality concentration.

According to the official field story, Portland has **nine players from the top 25** in the Rolex Rankings:

- **Jeeno Thitikul** at **No. 2**
- **Haeran Ryu** at **No. 3**
- **Lottie Woad** at **No. 5**
- **Ruoning Yin** at **No. 8**
- **Mao Saigo** at **No. 16**
- **Lauren Coughlin** at **No. 17**
- **Esther Henseleit** at **No. 18**
- **Aki Iwai** at **No. 19**
- **Hye-Jin Choi** at **No. 25**

That is enough star power on its own.

Then the LPGA adds that the field includes **seven winners from the 2026 LPGA season**, including **Ryu**, **Thitikul**, **Woad**, **Lauren Coughlin**, **Celine Boutier**, **Yana Wilson**, and **Gina Kim**.

So this is not one of those weeks where the ranking sheet looks decent but half the field is mostly trying to locate itself again after a major.

There are active winners all over this thing.

## The Amateur Angle Is Actually Worth Your Attention

The amateur part of the field is not decorative.

The LPGA's field breakdown says Portland also gets three serious amateur champion exemptions:

- **Maria Jose Marin**, the **Augusta National Women's Amateur** champion
- **Farah O'Keefe**, the **NCAA individual champion**
- **Valentine Delon**, the **Women's Amateur Championship** winner

That is a genuinely fun trio.

We already covered [O'Keefe's NCAA title run and immediate jump into major-championship relevance](/blog/farah-okeefe-ncaa-title-us-womens-open-2026/), and Marin was already part of [our U.S. Women's Open amateurs piece](/blog/us-womens-open-amateurs-riviera-2026/). Portland now gives that broader women's-golf pipeline story another useful stage instead of making everybody wait for the next major before pretending elite amateurs exist.

The LPGA also says there are **21 rookies** in the field, which means this week has a lot more upward-motion energy than the usual veteran-placeholder reading people slap onto late-summer tour stops.

## The Opening-Round Groups Are Not Bad Either

The official **featured-groups** post from **Wednesday, August 12** says the opening rounds will spotlight a few useful trios, including one built around **Ruoning Yin**, another around **Haeran Ryu**, another featuring **Jeeno Thitikul** and **Lottie Woad**, and a separate group of **Lexi Thompson**, **Chanettee Wannasaen**, and **Lindy Duncan**.

That Lexi group has its own side note because the LPGA also notes **Thompson** announced this week that she is **pregnant with her first child**, due in **February 2027**, while continuing a limited playing schedule.

That does not make Portland "the Lexi event," obviously.
It just adds one more recognizable thread to a tournament that already had enough names.

## Why This Week Matters

The best thing about the Portland setup is that it gives the LPGA a chance to keep the season feeling connected after the **AIG Women's Open** instead of resetting the whole conversation from scratch.

You still have:

- **Ryu** trying to stay scorching hot
- **Thitikul** keeping weekly pressure on the top end
- **Woad** showing up as one of the season's most interesting young stars
- **Aki Iwai** returning to defend
- and a trio of amateur champions trying to turn a sponsor exemption into a real week

That is plenty.

We already argued in [our broader growth piece](/blog/lpga-growth-finally-looks-structural-2026/) that women's golf looks stronger when the underlying systems and storylines stop feeling isolated. Portland fits that same point. It is not a random come-down week. It is a continuation week.
