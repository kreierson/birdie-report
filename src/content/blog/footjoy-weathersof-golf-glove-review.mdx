FootJoy's current **WeatherSof prior-generation** page is refreshingly direct.

The product story is built around:

- **advanced performance leather** in key stress areas
- **PowerNet mesh** across the knuckles
- **ComforTab** closure
- **FiberSof** material for fit, softness, and grip

That tells you exactly what this glove is.

It is not a full-premium leather flex.

It is a **hybrid budget glove** trying to balance durability, comfort, and price without pretending it belongs in the same emotional category as premium cabretta gloves.
