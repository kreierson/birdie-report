The **Galaxy Watch Ultra can put golf yardages on your wrist**, but buying the watch is only part of the decision. You also need a compatible phone and a golf app whose watch features match how you play.

My buying advice: if you already want an Android smartwatch, check the golf workflow before paying for a second device. If golf is the only reason you want a watch, compare a dedicated option before committing to an app subscription.

This is **research-based coverage**, checked against Samsung and Golf Pad documentation on **October 12, 2026**. I have not tested this watch on a course, measured its GPS accuracy or timed its battery. The setup below describes the provider's instructions, not a hands-on test.

## First, Check the Model and Your Phone

The [Samsung US product page](https://www.samsung.com/us/watches/galaxy-watch-ultra/) describes the **2025 Galaxy Watch Ultra**, with 64 GB storage. Do not assume a listing with “Ultra” in its title is the same year or condition. The page showed **$649.99 before trade-in** when checked; that is a dated manufacturer reference, not an Amazon price or a promised discount.

According to [Samsung's compatibility guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004668/), Watch Ultra supports **Android 11 or later**, including compatible non-Samsung phones. It does **not support iPhone**. Features can vary with phone, region and carrier, so confirm the functions you actually need before buying.

The practical order is phone first, golf app second, seller listing third. A good deal on the wrong combination is still the wrong combination.

## Golf Pad: What Is Free and What Needs Premium?

Golf Pad is one documented route to golf use on Wear OS. It is not a Samsung-native golf feature, and this guide does not rank it against every competing app.

The provider's [Wear OS feature guide](https://support.golfpadgps.com/support/solutions/articles/2850-what-functionality-is-available-in-the-golf-pad-app-for-android-wear-os-watches-) separates the watch functions this way:

- **Free:** yardages to the front, center and rear of the green.
- **Premium:** watch maps, hazard distances, shot and club tracking, and standalone use of the watch's GPS.

That distinction matters if “golf GPS” means more to you than a basic green distance. Check the platform-specific feature list; phone features do not automatically carry over to the watch. Also check that you are reading instructions for the current Golf Pad app, rather than Golf Pad Classic.

[Golf Pad's pricing FAQ](https://golfpadgps.com/faq) listed Premium at **$29.99–$49.99 per year** when checked. Confirm the plan and renewal price in your own account. Include that recurring cost in the comparison with a dedicated golf watch.

## Setup and the Limits of Phone-Free Golf

The [official Wear OS setup guide](https://support.golfpadgps.com/support/solutions/articles/2849-setting-up-golf-pad-watch-app-on-android-wear-os) calls for **Wear OS 4 or newer**, the Android phone app, and Bluetooth pairing. Install Golf Pad on the watch, then enable Smartwatch Sync in the phone app's preferences.

For standalone use, the [provider's separate instructions](https://support.golfpadgps.com/support/solutions/articles/6000064171-can-i-use-my-gps-equipped-wear-os-watch-without-a-phone-in-standalone-mode-) say to update both apps, enable watch location, and turn on the watch-GPS option under Smartwatch Sync. You still **start and finish the round on the phone**. Standalone lets the watch supply location during play; it does not remove the phone from every step.

Before relying on it for a round, confirm that your course loads, the watch connects and the features you expect are in your plan. Do that before reaching the first tee. Troubleshooting Bluetooth while three groups watch you is a lousy warm-up.

## Battery Claims Are Not a Golf-Round Test

Samsung advertises up to **100 hours in Power Saving Mode** and **48 hours in Exercise Power Saving Mode**. Those are different operating modes, not measured Golf Pad runtime. They do not establish how many golf rounds you will get with your chosen screen, GPS and app settings.

I would not choose between this watch and a dedicated golf model by comparing headline battery numbers alone. This review has no controlled endurance or accuracy comparison to support a winner.

Samsung also lists dual-frequency GPS and 10 ATM/IP68 water resistance. Water resistance is conditional; follow Samsung's use and care restrictions rather than treating a depth rating as an unrestricted waterproof guarantee.

## A Buying Checklist Before You Open Amazon

1. **Confirm the exact year and model.** This article's manufacturer reference is the 2025 version. Read the actual listing, not just the search-result title.
2. **Check condition, seller, included band and return terms.** Renewed and new offers can appear together. The link below opens a search, not a vetted individual offer.
3. **Choose the golf features you need.** Basic green yardages and phone-free GPS have different plan requirements in the documented Golf Pad workflow.
4. **Budget for the app separately.** A watch purchase does not imply that a third-party subscription is included.
5. **Decide whether you want a smartwatch or primarily a golf tool.** If everyday apps justify the purchase for you, golf can be an added use. If they do not, compare the golf workflow and total cost before paying a premium.
