The **FedEx St. Jude Championship** is technically where the playoffs start.

The nastier truth is that the real fight in **Memphis** begins at **No. 49**.

Official **PGA TOUR** preview, standings, and playoff-scenarios pages checked on **Tuesday, August 11, 2026** show **69 of the top 70** players in the **FedExCup** are in the field at **TPC Southwind**, with **Daniel Berger** the only qualifier not playing. The same official pages show **Maverick McNealy** sitting **50th** at **805.974 points**, only **five points behind Keith Mitchell** at **No. 49**, while **Harris English** is **51st** at **778.923** and **Jackson Koivun** has barely survived into Memphis at **No. 70**.

So yes, **Scottie Scheffler** opens the week at **No. 1** and **Rory McIlroy** opens at **No. 12**.
But if you are looking for the sharpest pressure, it is lower down the board.

This piece is based on the PGA TOUR's official **FedEx St. Jude Championship First Look**, the official **FedExCup standings page**, and the TOUR's **August 10 playoff-scenarios update**, all checked again on Tuesday, August 11, 2026. No pretending I got access to the bubble room whiteboard at TPC Southwind.

For the run-in to this week, read [our Wyndham final-round piece on Michael Brennan and Beau Hossler](/blog/beau-hossler-michael-brennan-wyndham-sunday-2026/), [our Monday recap of Brennan's win and playoff jump](/blog/michael-brennan-wins-wyndham-playoffs-2026/), and [our Jason Day column on how ugly the missed-playoff math already got](/blog/jason-day-missed-playoffs-health-opinion-2026/).

## The Top-70 Story Ended Sunday. The Top-50 Story Just Started.

The TOUR's official first-look preview makes the field part simple enough:

- **69** players at **TPC Southwind**
- **Daniel Berger** the only eligible player not competing
- **top 50 after this week** advance to the **BMW Championship**
- the same **top 50** also lock up spots in the **2027 Signature Events**

That last point is why this week matters beyond the trophy.

Getting to **BMW** is not just a nice little second-playoff-week bonus.
It is a schedule upgrade.
It is access.
It is a much easier life next season.

That is why the official scenarios page is more interesting than most preview copy.
It tells you exactly how thin the margin is.

## McNealy Is the Current Line, but the Crowd Behind Him Is the Story

Checked Tuesday, August 11, the official standings show:

- **Keith Mitchell** at **No. 49** with **811.435**
- **Maverick McNealy** at **No. 50** with **805.974**
- **Harris English** at **No. 51** with **778.923**
- **Max Homa** at **No. 52** with **767.387**
- **Jordan Spieth** at **No. 54** with **761.621**

The TOUR's scenario sheet then gives the ugly part.

According to that page, the weakest finish that would still give each player a mathematical path to jump past where McNealy currently sits is:

- **English:** solo **31st**
- **Homa:** **two-way T25**
- **Sungjae Im:** **two-way T24**
- **Spieth:** solo **23rd**

That is a much meaner playoff opener than the generic branding suggests.

It is not enough to play decent.
It is not enough to be recognizable.
It is not enough to "get four rounds."

You need a finish with teeth.

## Koivun and Brennan Are the Two Best Reminders That the Board Still Moves

The first-look preview also notes that **Michael Brennan** earned his way into the playoffs for the first time after winning **Wyndham**.

We already covered the cruel version of that jump in [our Monday Brennan piece](/blog/michael-brennan-wins-wyndham-playoffs-2026/), but the official TOUR recap still makes the point worth repeating: Brennan started Wyndham at **No. 105**, won, and arrived in Memphis at **No. 47**.

That is not bubbling in.
That is kicking the structure in the ribs.

Then there is **Jackson Koivun**.

The official first-look page says Koivun matched **Tiger Woods'** record by needing only **five starts** to reach the **FedExCup Playoffs**, but the official standings and scenarios pages also show how exposed he still is. Koivun is **No. 70** at **581.462 points**, only a few points clear of the players behind him, and the TOUR's own scenarios page says he would need at least a **two-way tie for seventh** to have a mathematical route into the top 50.

That is the difference between "made the playoffs" and "actually changed your life."

## Big Names Are Sweating This Too

The first-look preview is obviously built around the stars.
That part is fair.

**Scheffler** is No. 1.
**McIlroy** is chasing another **FedExCup** from outside the top 10 for the first time since **2021**.
**Tommy Fleetwood** is back as defending season-long champion.
**Justin Rose** is defending this tournament.

But the stronger week-to-week pressure is on the names below them.

Official TOUR pages checked Tuesday show **Shane Lowry** at **No. 62**, with the scenarios page saying he needs at least a **two-way tie for 11th** to have a mathematical chance to jump the current top-50 line. **Michael Kim** at **No. 61** needs at least a **three-way tie for 12th**. Even once you are safely inside the current playoff field, the line does not stop moving.

That is why Memphis is not just about who wins.
It is about who keeps a better version of next year.

## Bottom Line

Checked on **Tuesday, August 11, 2026**, the official **PGA TOUR** preview, standings, and playoff-scenarios pages show the **FedEx St. Jude Championship** opening with **69 players**, **Daniel Berger** absent, **Maverick McNealy** holding the final **BMW Championship** spot at **No. 50**, and a pile of recognizable names needing something materially better than a shrug.

The playoff field is already set.

The real drama now is whether players like **McNealy**, **English**, **Homa**, **Spieth**, **Lowry**, and **Koivun** can turn one week in Memphis into a much easier **2027**.
