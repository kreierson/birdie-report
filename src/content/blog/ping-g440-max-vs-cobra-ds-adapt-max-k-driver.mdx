## Forgiveness: PING Wins the Cleaner Category, Cobra Stays Close Enough to Matter

The **G440 Max** still has the safer forgiveness reputation.

It is the calmer answer for:

- mixed-face contact
- ugly-tee-shot anxiety
- golfers who want help without buying a head that feels like a correction device

But the **Cobra** is not getting embarrassed here.

Cobra explicitly sells the MAX-K as the most forgiving head in the family, and the **10,000 MOI** line is doing exactly the job it is supposed to do: tell normal golfers this is the head built for their messy contact.

So the honest split is:

## Adjustability: Cobra Is More Interesting Than a Discounted Max Head Usually Gets to Be

This is where the MAX-K punches back hardest.

The **FutureFit33** sleeve gives Cobra a more ambitious tuning story than the usual "turn loft up or down and call it a day" setup.

That matters if:

- launch is close but not perfect
- lie angle needs help
- you want more setup freedom without jumping to a fitter-only niche head

The **PING** still offers a cleaner mainstream tuning path through its hosel and back-weight story, but Cobra's system is the more interesting toolset on paper.

That is a real part of the value case.
