This is one of the most normal golf-watch decisions you can make.

The **Garmin Approach S44** is the nicer watch.

The **Garmin Approach S12** is the smarter buy for most golfers.

That is the whole page.

If you are comparing **Garmin Approach S44 vs Garmin Approach S12**, I would buy the **S12** first unless you already know you want the brighter screen, cleaner visuals, and more premium Garmin feel badly enough to pay the extra **$100**.

This comparison is **research-based** and built from the current official **Garmin** product pages checked on **July 27, 2026**, plus the surrounding Birdie Report GPS-watch cluster already in the repo. No fake "I wore both for 40 rounds and achieved wrist enlightenment" routine.

## Why the S12 Gets the Recommendation

The **S12** wins because it handles the important job without charging you extra for being prettier.

Garmin's current S12 page still leans on:

- **$199.99**
- **42,000** preloaded CourseView maps
- **up to 30 hours** in GPS mode
- **Big Numbers mode**
- hazard and dogleg distances

That is a very clean pitch.

The S12 is for golfers who want:

- quick front-middle-back yardages
- simple hazard context
- big battery life
- buttons instead of touchscreen fiddling

That buyer exists in huge numbers.

And that buyer usually does not need to spend **$100 more** just because another watch looks nicer on the wrist.

If you already use a [rangefinder](/blog/best-rangefinders-2026/) and mostly want fast reference numbers without another device getting needy, the **S12** still makes a ton of sense.

## Why the S44 Still Has a Real Case

The **S44** is the better watch experience.

That matters more than minimalist golf-tech purists want to admit.

Garmin's current S44 product page highlights:

- **$299.99**
- **1.2-inch AMOLED touchscreen**
- **43,000** preloaded courses
- **hazard view**
- **Green View**
- **up to 15 hours** of battery in GPS mode

That makes the S44 a lot easier to enjoy hole after hole.

The S44 is the one you buy when you are tired of budget-golf-watch energy and want something that feels cleaner, brighter, and more modern every time you look down.

It is not the better value.

It is the better watch.

That is an important difference.
