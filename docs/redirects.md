The site does not publish an RSS or Atom feed. `astro.config.mjs` builds the sitemap from remaining articles, so a removed post drops out of the sitemap when its MDX file is deleted.

Redirect targets must be live articles. Do not point a redirect at a phase-2 hold slug in `data/consolidation-holds.json`, and do not chain a redirect into another redirect.
