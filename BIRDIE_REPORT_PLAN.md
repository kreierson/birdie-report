# The Birdie Report operating plan

The current objective is sustainable affiliate commissions of at least $10,000 per month, followed by continued growth. Revenue and ranking outcomes are uncertain; targets are not forecasts.

The current operating instructions are [Revenue operations](docs/REVENUE_OPERATIONS.md), [Autonomous SEO](docs/AUTONOMOUS_SEO.md), and [Content rules](CONTENT_RULES.md). The earlier speculative traffic, keyword-volume and conversion projections have been retired because they were not measured account results.

The daily operator collects complete analytics windows, refreshes authenticated Amazon observations when available, and creates a private revenue scorecard. It prioritizes factual accuracy, existing qualified traffic, useful buying decisions, and reliable measurement. It publishes only after normal build checks, actual browser QA and production verification.

Private baselines and experiment records live in ignored reports/seo; they must not be included in this public repository. The source-controlled runbook describes methods and limits, not private account data.
